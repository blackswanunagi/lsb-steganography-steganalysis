

### **1. Visual / Perceptual Steganalysis**

* Based on **human observation**
* Detects visible or audible anomalies

**Examples:**

* Visual inspection of images
* Listening to audio files
* Zooming, contrast enhancement

📌 *Simple but limited*

---

### **2. Statistical Steganalysis**

* Uses **statistical properties** of the media
* Looks for abnormal distributions

**Examples:**

* LSB distribution analysis
* Histogram analysis
* Entropy measurement
* Chi-square test

📌 *Most commonly used*

---

### **3. Structural Steganalysis**

* Analyzes the **file structure and format**
* Detects inconsistencies in headers or metadata

**Examples:**

* Image/audio header analysis
* File size anomalies
* Format violations

📌 *Effective against poor implementations*

---

### **4. Transform Domain Steganalysis**

* Works in **frequency domains**
* Detects hidden data in transformed coefficients

**Examples:**

* DCT analysis (JPEG)
* DWT analysis (Wavelets)

📌 *Used for compressed media*

---

### **5. Machine Learning–Based Steganalysis**

* Uses **AI models** to classify media
* Learns hidden patterns automatically

**Examples:**

* SVM classifiers
* Neural Networks
* Deep Learning (CNNs)

📌 *High accuracy, complex*

---

### **6. Blind vs Targeted Steganalysis**

* **Blind (Universal):** no knowledge of the embedding method
* **Targeted (Specific):** designed for a known technique

📌 *Important classification criterion*

---

➡️ *Steganalysis techniques can be visual, statistical, structural, transform-based, or AI-driven, and may be blind or targeted.*
